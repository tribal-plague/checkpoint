Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Checkpoint is a desktop app for Windows that backs up PC game save files. It runs on your computer. There is no Checkpoint server: the developer does not receive, store or see any of your data.

Data Checkpoint uses

  • Game save files on your PC, which Checkpoint copies into backup files.
  • Your Google account email address, if you sign in with Google. It is shown in the app so you know which account is in use, and used to keep each account's backups separate.
  • Files Checkpoint creates in your Google Drive, if you sign in. Checkpoint uses the drive.file permission, which only gives access to files and folders the app created itself (the Checkpoint folder). It cannot read, change or delete anything else in your Drive.

Where data is stored

  • Backups are stored on your PC (by default in Documents\Checkpoint Backups) and, if you sign in, in your own Google Drive.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored on your PC only, encrypted for your Windows user account.

Sharing

Checkpoint does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data goes only between your PC and your own Google Drive, directly over Google's secure APIs. Checkpoint contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Checkpoint's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Use Sign out in Checkpoint to remove the sign-in token from your PC.
  • Revoke Checkpoint's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Checkpoint folder in Google Drive and the local backup folder to remove all backups.

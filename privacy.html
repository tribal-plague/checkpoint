Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Checkpoint is a desktop app for Windows that backs up PC game save files and keeps them in step between your computers. This page explains what it stores and where.

Data Checkpoint uses

  • Game save files on your PC, which Checkpoint copies into backup files.
  • Your Google account's name and email address, from Sign in with Google. They identify your account so your backups are kept separate from everyone else's, and are shown in the app. Checkpoint does not get access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Small details about your PCs and games: the device name you choose (for example "Desktop") and each game's name and save locations, so your other PCs know where to restore saves.

Where data is stored

  • Every backup is stored on your PC (by default in Documents\Checkpoint Backups).
  • When you're signed in, backups are also stored in Checkpoint's cloud storage, run on Supabase. Access rules allow each signed-in account to read and change only its own backups. Checkpoint keeps the latest backups of each game (5 by default) and removes older ones.
  • Your sign-in session is stored on your PC only, encrypted for your Windows user account.

Sharing

Checkpoint does not sell or share your data, and contains no ads, analytics or tracking. Data goes only between your PC and Checkpoint's cloud storage over encrypted connections. As the operator, the developer could technically access stored files, but does not look at them except to help you when you ask.

Removing your data

  • Use Sign out in Checkpoint to remove the sign-in session from your PC.
  • Email the address below to have your account and all its cloud backups deleted.
  • Delete the local backup folder to remove the backups on your PC.

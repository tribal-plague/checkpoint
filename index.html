Backs up when you quit

Close a game and its saves are backed up a few seconds later. A scheduled backup catches anything else.

Your Google Drive

Every backup is kept on your PC and in a Checkpoint folder in your own Drive. Works offline and uploads later.

Safety copy first

Before anything is restored, your current saves are copied aside, so a restore can always be undone.

Spots conflicts

Played on both PCs without syncing? Checkpoint notices and lets you choose which save to keep.

Version history

Keeps your recent backups for every game, so you can roll back to before that bad decision.

Learns any game

Thousands of games are recognised automatically. For the rest, Learn mode finds the save files for you.

How it works

Three steps. About two minutes.

No accounts to create and nothing to configure.

Install and sign in

Run the installer and sign in with Google, or use Checkpoint without an account and keep backups on this PC.

Play like you always do

Checkpoint finds your games and backs them up every time you quit. You don't have to remember anything.

Continue on your other PC

Install it on the laptop, sign in with the same account, and click Get latest save on any game.

Privacy

Your saves stay yours.

There's no Checkpoint server. Your backups go straight from your PC to your own Google Drive, and nowhere else.

  • Checkpoint can only see the files it creates. The rest of your Drive is off limits.
  • Your Google sign-in is stored encrypted, on your PC only.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. Ever.
  • Revoke access any time from your Google account.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Checkpoint free?

Yes. Checkpoint is free, with no ads and no subscription. Backups use your own Google Drive storage, and most save files are tiny.

Which games does it work with?

It finds games installed through Steam, Epic Games and GOG and recognises thousands of them from a community save-location database. For anything else, add the game and use Learn mode: play, save, quit, and Checkpoint works out where the saves live.

Do I need a Google account?

No. Choose Use without signing in and backups stay on your PC. You can also point Checkpoint at a folder that OneDrive or Dropbox syncs. Sign in later and your games and backups come with you.

Can it overwrite or delete my saves?

Checkpoint never restores without asking, refuses to restore while the game is running, and always makes a safety copy of your current saves first. Old backups it cleans up go to your Drive's trash, where Google keeps them for 30 days.

What happens when I'm offline?

Backups are always saved on your PC first. They upload to Google Drive automatically the next time you're online.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

That message appears for new apps that haven't built up a download history with Microsoft yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Checkpoint installs just for your Windows user, so it never asks for administrator rights.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall it from Windows Settings like any app. To revoke Google access, sign out in Checkpoint or visit your Google account permissions. Your backups stay in your Drive until you delete them.
